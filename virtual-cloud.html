THE VIRTUAL CLOUD

Codesphere: The World’s First Virtual Cloud

Our Virtual Cloud Provider (VCP) technology abstracts any infrastructure— from Hyperscalers to On-Prem—into a unified, enterprise-ready platform. Eliminate vendor lock-in and scale your software without functional compromises.

WHY A VIRTUAL CLOUD

End the choice between lock-in and limitation.

In today’s world, organizations often face a painful choice: accept the rigid “lock-in” of major Hyperscalers to gain high-end features, or move to local clouds and lose the operative depth required for state-of-the-art applications, AI and scale.

Codesphere’s virtual cloud platform addresses the root causes for lock-in with Hyperscalers. These are typically related to the availability of provider managed services, scalability and convenience factors. By abstracting these factors and making them an integral part of a layer on top of cloud providers or any other infrastructure, Codesphere enables true independence and interoperability.

Much like Airbnb owns no hotels, Codesphere owns no servers; instead, we upgrade your existing hardware or cloud provider into a standardized, high-performance development and operations environment.

KEY BENEFITS

One platform. Every infrastructure.

A unified operating layer that delivers governance, portability and operational efficiency across your entire cloud and on-premise estate.

INFRASTRUCTURE AGNOSTIC

Run seamlessly on Hyperscalers, European providers, or your own On-Premise data centers.

STANDARDIZED GOVERNANCE

Apply consistent security and compliance rules across your entire hybrid or multi-cloud landscape through a single unified layer.

ZERO LOCK-IN

Move workloads between different providers without rewriting code or losing cloud-native capabilities.

OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY

Reduce the need for specialized Kubernetes expertise with a platform that abstracts complexity for your developers.

AI READINESS
