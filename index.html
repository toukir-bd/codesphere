MEET THE VIRTUAL CLOUD

A sovereign environment across on-prem, cloud, and hybrid infrastructure.

Codesphere owns no servers, yet upgrades any infrastructure into an enterprise-ready cloud, vendor-neutral, portable, and sovereign by design.

So everything companies need to develop, deploy, and manage their applications is unified. Our platform can be installed on any infrastructure, making it independent from specific cloud providers. This way, you can easily switch between clouds (or on-prem) if needed.

  • Portable across on-prem, cloud & hybrid
